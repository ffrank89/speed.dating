# 0017: Microgames come from moments in the date

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [dates.md](../design/dates.md), [perks.md](../design/perks.md)

## Decision

Microgames are WarioWare-style tests of a few seconds taken from the date itself (light her cigarette, catch her drink). Attributes and perks change difficulty. Each is its own scene with start(difficulty, modifiers) in and finished(success) out. The first build needs 3.

## Why

Ties the skill test to the fiction; a shared contract lets each one be built and tested alone.

## Not chosen

Generic minigames unrelated to the date.

## Revisit when

Microgames interrupt the flow of dialogue too much.
