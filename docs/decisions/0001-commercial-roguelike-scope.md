# 0001: Target: a small commercial roguelike

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [core-loop.md](../design/core-loop.md)

## Decision

Build an inexpensive commercial roguelike. The first build centres on replayability. Start small, as Balatro did.

## Why

A small, replayable core is cheaper to make than a long story game, and roguelikes let a little content go a long way.

## Not chosen

A linear story-driven dating sim; a jam-sized one-off.

## Revisit when

The first build is playable and we're deciding scope for a demo or release.
