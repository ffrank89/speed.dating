# 0033: First build: type crit/fumble lines instead of unique abilities

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md), [girls.md](../design/girls.md)

## Decision

Girls have no unique abilities in the first build. When her type is strong or weak against the boss she crits or fumbles and says a written line for that boss.

## Why

Cheapest version that delivers the joke ("she went to Yale?"); abilities come later.

## Not chosen

One triggered ability per girl from the start.

## Revisit when

Fights feel the same every time once the jokes are known.
