# 0034: Bosses are full illustrations; girls fight as pixel sprites

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md)

## Decision

The boss is drawn as a full illustration (like the girls' date poses) with idle, attack, hurt and defeated poses. The girls in the fight stay pixel sprites.

## Why

Higher impact for the big moment of each chapter.

## Not chosen

Pixel boss 2–3× the girls' size, matching their style.

## Revisit when

The mixed styles look wrong once the first boss and girl sprites exist.
