# 0005: Days per chapter: about 5 actions

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md), [core-loop.md](../design/core-loop.md)

## Decision

Each chapter gives about 5 actions (to tune).

## Why

Scarce days force trade-offs between new dates, follow-ups, training and events.

## Not chosen

A full 30-day month per chapter.

## Revisit when

Players feel they can do everything (too many days) or nothing (too few).
