# 0040: Options above your attribute show a % chance

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-19
- **Affects:** [dates.md](../design/dates.md), [player.md](../design/player.md)

## Decision

At or above an option's requirement there's no roll. Below it, the button shows your odds, `attribute ÷ requirement` clamped to 5–95%. A hit gives the full reward; a miss loses points and gets a bad reaction.

## Why

Clear and tense (Disco Elysium). Players can see exactly what training buys them.

## Not chosen

Weaker rewards with no randomness; risky options turning into microgames.

## Revisit when

Players feel cheated by misses at high odds, or always pick the safe option.
