# 0011: Relationships: first date recruits, follow-ups raise Affection

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [dates.md](../design/dates.md), [girls.md](../design/girls.md), [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md)

## Decision

The first date recruits her if Affection is 1 or more. Follow-up dates with party members raise Affection (0–3).

## Why

Gives a breadth-vs-depth choice every date day.

## Not chosen

Affection fixed after one date; a long relationship meter.

## Revisit when

Follow-ups are never worth a day, or always are.
