# 0020: Player–girl synergy: Affection + preference match + perks

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [synergy.md](../design/synergy.md), [player.md](../design/player.md)

## Decision

Player–girl synergy is her Affection, plus how well the player's attributes match her preferences, plus player perks that interact with her tags.

## Why

Ties the dating half (Affection, attributes) and perks directly to fight strength.

## Not chosen

Affection only.

## Revisit when

One input dominates the others in practice.
