# 0037: Tinder and the party screen are phone apps over the calendar

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md)

## Decision

The Tinder screen and the party screen are phone-app screens that overlay the calendar instead of separate scenes.

## Why

Keeps the calendar as the hub and fits the dating-app theme.

## Not chosen

Separate full-screen scenes.

## Revisit when

The phone is too small for the party screen's synergy and lineup UI.
