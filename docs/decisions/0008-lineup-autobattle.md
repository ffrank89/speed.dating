# 0008: Fight format: pick 3 and their order, Super Auto Pets–style lineup

- **Status:** Superseded by 0030
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md), [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md)

## Decision

Before the fight the player picks which girls to bring (3 of up to 6) and their order. The fight is a lineup autobattle: girls attack in order, the boss hits whoever is in front, abilities fire on triggers.

## Why

A lineup is much cheaper to build than a grid, and order still gives a meaningful decision.

## Not chosen

A Teamfight Tactics–style grid (much more expensive to build); bringing the whole party.

## Revisit when

The fight feels too simple to be worth watching, or ordering doesn't matter.
