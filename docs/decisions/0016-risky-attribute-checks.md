# 0016: Attribute checks are risky, not locked

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [dates.md](../design/dates.md), [player.md](../design/player.md)

## Decision

Options above your attributes stay clickable but are weaker or riskier (Disco Elysium–style).

## Why

Keeps choices interesting at low stats and makes training feel like it lowers risk rather than unlocking buttons.

## Not chosen

Hard locks on options.

## Revisit when

Risk math is unreadable to players.
