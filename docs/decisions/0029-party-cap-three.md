# 0029: Party cap 3; break up to make room

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Supersedes 0014**
- **Affects:** [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md), [girls.md](../design/girls.md), [core-loop.md](../design/core-loop.md)

## Decision

The party holds up to 3 girls. Adding a 4th means breaking up with someone. You can bring fewer than 3 to the boss and leave a bad match out.

## Why

A small party keeps every girl important and every break-up meaningful, and means less art per run.

## Not chosen

Cap 6, bring 3 (0014). Cap 4–5.

## Revisit when

There's no real choice about who to bring because the party is almost always the lineup.
