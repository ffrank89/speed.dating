# 0015: Date variety: pooled first dates, written follow-ups

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [dates.md](../design/dates.md)

## Decision

First dates draw short exchanges from a per-girl pool. Follow-ups are written scenes in a fixed order. Microgames and perks add variety too.

## Why

Pools keep first dates fresh across runs; fixed follow-ups let each girl's story build.

## Not chosen

Fully written first dates; fully random follow-ups.

## Revisit when

First dates feel samey, or writing follow-ups is too slow.
