# 0036: Actions cost different numbers of days

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md)

## Decision

New dates take 2 or more days. Training takes 1. A break-up takes 1 day, but a break-up plus a new date takes only as long as the new date.

## Why

Makes new girls expensive, so follow-ups and training compete with them.

## Not chosen

Every action costs 1 day.

## Revisit when

Chapters run out of days before anything interesting happens.
