# 0030: Fight: up to 3 girls in order, front girl vs boss

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Supersedes 0008**
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md)

## Decision

Pick up to 3 girls and their order. The front girl attacks, then the boss attacks her; when she's knocked out the next steps up. Win when the boss hits 0 HP, lose when every girl is out.

## Why

Pure Super Auto Pets lineup: order matters a lot and it's easy to read and animate.

## Not chosen

Rounds where every girl attacks each turn; speed-based ticks.

## Revisit when

Back-line girls never act often enough for order to feel fair.
