# 0002: Core: dating feeds battle (Persona 5–style)

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [core-loop.md](../design/core-loop.md), [dates.md](../design/dates.md), [boss.md](../design/boss.md)

## Decision

Most play is dating. A passive boss fight then tests how well you understood the girls: their attributes, their fit with you and their match-up against the boss.

## Why

Linking the two gives every date a strategic purpose and gives the fight an emotional one.

## Not chosen

Dating only (no fight); a fight the player controls directly.

## Revisit when

Playtests show players skip dates to rush the fight, or ignore the fight entirely.
