# 0031: First build's boss: Thanksgiving

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md)

## Decision

The one boss in the first build is Thanksgiving. It's weak to Brainy and Sweet girls (they crit ×2) and strong against Wild girls (they fumble ×0.5). Artsy is neutral.

## Why

Meeting the family has obvious good and bad picks and lots of comedy.

## Not chosen

Concert / party (a good second boss to flip the picks); Valentine's Day; meeting the parents.

## Revisit when

We add a second boss; check the two charts contrast.
