# 0014: Party cap 6; break up to make room

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-11
- **Affects:** [girls.md](../design/girls.md), [calendar.md](../design/calendar.md)

## Decision

The party holds up to 6 girls. Adding a 7th means breaking up with someone.

## Why

Forces pruning, which is where builds get sharpened.

## Not chosen

Unlimited party; the oldest girl leaves automatically.

## Revisit when

Players never reach 6, or break-ups feel bad rather than strategic.
