# 0035: The fight is literal for now; what it represents is TBD

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-10-13
- **Affects:** [boss.md](../design/boss.md)

## Decision

For now the boss fight is a literal autobattle (HP, hits, knockouts). Whether it should represent something else (e.g. winning the family over) is open.

## Why

Lets us build and tune the fight without blocking on the fiction.

## Not chosen

Approval meter / Composure (winning them over) right away.

## Revisit when

Writing boss and bark lines, or when the literal fight feels at odds with the theme.
